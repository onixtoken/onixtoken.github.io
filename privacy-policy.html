Privacy Policy – Tap Jump

Last updated: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tap Jump mobile app handles data when you play the game. By using the app, you can play without creating an account.

1. Data we store on your device

  • The name or nickname you choose for the leaderboard.
  • Your high score, coins, avatars, maps, power-ups, missions, achievements, and sound/music preferences.
  • This data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

2. Leaderboard and Firebase

When you submit a score to the leaderboard, your nickname and score may be stored in Google Firebase Firestore and displayed publicly on the leaderboard. Do not use your full name, email, phone number, or any other personal information as your nickname.

The leaderboard is a public feature of the game. Published scores can be read by other users. To request correction or deletion of a score, contact the address below and provide the relevant nickname and score.

3. Google AdMob Ads

Tap Jump uses Google AdMob for interstitial and rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process technical data, advertising identifiers, general device information, app data, and ad interactions in order to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services.

Where required in your region, the app uses Google's consent process for ads. Depending on your choice and location, personalized or non-personalized ads may be shown. You can change your ad choices from your device settings or from the consent controls when they are displayed.

See also the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob policies.

4. Data we do not request

We do not request your email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or GPS location for the game's core features. The app has no account sign-in and does not sell personal data.

5. How data is used

  • To save your game progress and preferences.
  • To display your nickname and score on the leaderboard.
  • To deliver and measure ads through Google AdMob.
  • To protect the security, stability, and operation of the app.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Scores submitted to Firestore are kept for the operation of the leaderboard until deletion or correction is requested. To request deletion of a score or data linked to your nickname, contact us by email.

7. Children

Tap Jump is not specifically directed at children under 13. If the app is distributed to children, or if an audience that includes children is selected on Google Play, the relevant Google Play Families declarations and requirements must be completed, and appropriate ad settings must be used.

8. Security

Data sent to external services is transmitted over secure connections. No method of transmission or storage guarantees absolute security, but we use reasonable measures to protect data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app, services, or legal requirements change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
